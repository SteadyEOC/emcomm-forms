# Thanks

emcomm-forms is built from what working operators told us and showed us. Our thanks to:

- **Bob Tykulsker KM6SO**, for suggesting the "Save Data" route (write the form's own saved data and let the
  operator click Load Data), for his ICS-213 samples, and for explaining how Winlink Express and Pat
  handle form data.
- **Kevin Gray N9VPV** and his group, for Save Data files of seven Winlink Express forms from Emergency
  Communications Training Online (ETO) exercises, and for offering to test early versions.
- **Charles Congdon W1ZPB**, for his notes on custom Winlink forms from ETO's training.
- **Emergency Communications Training Online (ETO)**, whose weekly Winlink Thursday exercises produced
  many of the sample forms and will be our field test.
- **The Pat project** and the members of **winlink-programs-group**, for open code to learn from and for
  answering questions.

Want to help? Saved form files with made-up data, or a note on where a file from this library broke in your
program, are the most useful things. Open an issue.
